## Request text

Use the common body below with the relevant title list and additional question
from the routing table. Confirm the project/domain details before sending.

**Subject:** Permission request: edition-specific cover images for boardgame catalog and marketplace apps

Hello,

We are preparing a curated boardgame reference catalog used by our Rails
boardgame-marketplace and Django boardgame-exchange applications. We would like
to use official front-cover artwork for the products listed below to identify
games in search results, collection pages, game details and marketplace listings.
These uses may be commercial. Stock covers would be labeled as representative
artwork; sellers' photographs remain separate.

Could you confirm which of the following uses you authorize, and any conditions?

1. Displaying the selected covers in both commercial and noncommercial catalog
   and marketplace pages.
2. Hosting resized WebP copies on our image host/CDN, with images contained within
   320 × 320 thumbnails and 1200 × 1200 detail views. We would preserve the entire
   cover, avoid upscaling, and retain required attribution and source links.
3. Allowing our consuming applications to cache or self-host those same renditions.
4. Separately, including those renditions in optional downloadable catalog bundles
   for other consuming applications. Please indicate if permission is limited
   to the two named applications or URL-based delivery instead.

Please identify the covered edition/language/SKU, provide your preferred asset
source and credit line, and note any limitations on territory, duration, delivery
method or onward use. We also welcome a designated contact for correction or
withdrawal requests. If another rights holder must authorize a pictured edition,
please point us to them.

Products and edition questions: [insert the row below].

Thank you.
